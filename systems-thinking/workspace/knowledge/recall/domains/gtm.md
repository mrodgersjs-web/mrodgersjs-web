## Positioning (anti-median, category, wedge)

- Position as a **Revenue Intelligence Graph OS**, not an “AI automation agency” (already the median). Shift: from “we build AI automations” to “we install the OS your business uses to sense, decide, execute, and learn.” Category claim: private agentic OS that turns signals into scored opportunities, executable workflows, revenue-recovery loops, and improving intelligence. [Card: RIG 10X Strategy Card — Revenue Intelligence Graph Operating System]
- Rodgers brand essence candidate: “I don’t advise on AI — I operate businesses with it.” Proof already on the table: 100+ agents in production; healthcare / enterprise / startup / fleet; offer names that must not compress to “we help companies use AI” (Kolmogorov originality fail). Positioning artifacts: essence, category, one-sentence, enemy, hero, villain, proof mechanism, memory phrase. [Card: Rodgers Intelligence Full Rebuild Master Prompt — Strategy, Brand, Website, Offers, GTM, Research, and RIG Deviation Engine Implementation](https://rodgersintelligence.com/)
- Research gate: benefit-based and user/surrogate positioning beat feature-based on favourability, differentiation, and credibility (Fuchs & Diamantopoulos, 2010). Feature-only is auto-flagged. Score positioning on five dimensions (Shahid 2023; Blankson & Kalafatis 2004); all ≥ 75. Generate 10+ variants, score, A/B against competitor maps, human-approve. [Card: RIG OS Go-To-Market Doctrine V2: Research-Backed Autonomous GTM Engine]
- AI wedge (3 sentences + 4 incumbent tests): (1) incumbent was built when constraint X was unavoidable; (2) AI eliminates X by mechanism Y; (3) without X, pricing/architecture Z no longer makes sense. Then: incumbent revenue model, human/technical constraint, what matching you would force them to sacrifice, whether that sacrifice hurts the P&L. If they can copy you without business-model pain, you have a feature, not a wedge. Harvey: $200M ARR in 4 years by selling the legal **deliverable**, not search-volume. Seat-based CRM is a legacy tax once agents populate the system. [Card: The AI Wedge: 3 Steps to Break Into a Mature Software Category](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OopKjnvniV8)
- Value-prop syntax: Who + need + unique solution; 3Us (Unworkable, Unavoidable, Underserved) and 3Ds (Defensible, Disruptive, Discontinuous). Do not say faster/better/cheaper — state a 10× gain/pain so adoption pain is dominated. [Card: Value Props: Create a Product People Will Actually Buy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q8d9uuO1Cf4)
- Gatekeeper / reactance (positioning of access): qualify the buyer; “you’re a great fit” after selectivity creates identity-commitment; access scarcity beats quantity/time scarcity because it stacks reactance + social proof. Loss frame ≈ 2.25× gain frame (prospect theory). [Card: This ONE Psychological Trigger Gets Clients to Chase You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VjyDi69NbVc)

## Metrics, formulas, and the 90-day install

```
CAC = TotalSalesAndMarketingSpend / NewCustomersAcquired
LTV = ARPA × GrossMargin × AvgLifespan
Target LTV/CAC ≥ 3.0; alert < 2.0 (pause worst channels); kill < 1.0 (halt paid)
SalesVelocity = (QualifiedOpps × AvgDealValue × WinRate) / CycleLength
  raise velocity by tightening qualification, not loosening it
CAC Payback = CAC / Monthly Gross Profit per Customer
Gross Margin = (Revenue − Delivery Cost) / Revenue
  Delivery = founder + contractor + software/API + research + PM + support
```
