## Mention graphs, tagging, MentionFish

- Best place to mention **multiple** people is usually a **comment**, not the original post. Post stays clean; comment is the distribution hook. **Do:** mention only when directly relevant; ask each person a *specific* question; add context before the mention; reply substantively; keep native (avoid outbound links in the original post). **Don’t:** tag a huge list (looks spammy). Cap **3–5 people**, grouped by why they should care. **Card: LinkedIn Tagging Strategy for Viral Reach** (`3bf2915c`, `b81e32f9`)
