## Pipeline rhythm (LAPS) and sales velocity

- Run a weekly **LAPS** loop — Leads, Appointments, Presentations, Sales — as the whole-business sales cadence, not a one-off close technique. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Appointment pitch is a six-beat paragraph (fits a text/email): **Name, Same, Fame, Pain, Aim, Game**, then a diary link. Example Pain/Aim: “over 50% of the population want to write a book but less than 2% ever achieve that” → platform that lets 10× more starters finish. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Expect attrition at booking: **out of every 12 leads, less than half set an appointment**. Qualify on the signal-of-interest form (current situation, desired outcome, solutions already in mind) only after volume appears — early-stage, take the meetings.   Levers: sharper ICP / AI Opportunity Report (opps); audit → sprint → embedded operator ladder (ACV); proof + published pricing (win rate); diagnostic + buyer-specific pages (cycle). ## Discovery, presentation, close

- Permission into process after 2–3 minutes of rapport (name, common interest, smile, shared people): “chitchat, or I ask a series of questions and give recommendations — which do you want?” Most pick the process. Then hit the same bases either way: **present situation** (what is less than perfect) → **prize** (desired outcome / the movie they play of ownership) — people buy the prize, not the product (Ferrari-in-storage test). [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Sequence that separates low-price low-conversion from high-price high-conversion: **insights (frameworks/research that are not the product) → method → solution**. Do not pitch the offer first. Keep a rehearsed “golf bag” of insights/methods keyed to prize and problem. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Always put a **visual aid** in front of the buyer (brochure, scaled model, slide deck). The card’s claim: executives will sign multi-million contracts off two visuals; talking-only presentations lose deals they should win. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- After the offer, **do not play conversational tennis** (Q–A–Q–A). Catch every concern, hold them, answer as one grouped block, then complete (paperwork, CRM, onboarding emails) if they are ready. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Follow-up is not optional: the card claims it can **double the business**. Peak commit window is the **first 48 hours**. Plan **at least seven** creative/relevant touches (new testimonial, new data, special, callback to a reason they named). Speed: lead in Monday 10:00 → follow up **10:01**; “play it cool until Friday” is forbidden. After 6–7 touches, run a reactivation: “have you given up on [goal]?” + opt-out; if still interested, send a ScoreApp assessment or Excel calculator and recycle them as a lead. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Improvement loop: record every presentation with an AI notetaker → transcript → ChatGPT to restock the golf bag **and** to extract common themes of meetings that closed vs bombed. [Card: 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tVLnzcoM5LE)
- Five-phase AI overlay on the same motion: (1) prospecting as “tap on the shoulder — do you have this problem?” (2) ICP lists via an AI CRM (card names Manis.AI) that clones current customers, names the actual buyer, and dumps social/contact rows; later phases use AI for objection handling so humans stay on relationship. ## Written replies, objections, and the open loop

- Canonical reply arc (do not chase): **Wound → Mirror → Autonomy → Qualification → Open Loop**. The recipient should feel seen, evaluated, safe, challenged, pulled forward. Anti-patterns that fail the gate: “let me know your thoughts,” “just checking in,” “would love to chat,” “happy to help.” [Card: RIG Email Reply Scoring Algorithm: Wound, Autonomy, Mirror, Close]
- Score before send:

  ```
  EmailReplyScore =
    0.20*WoundIdentification + 0.15*Specificity + 0.15*Restraint
    + 0.15*AutonomyPreservation + 0.12*MirrorAccuracy
    + 0.10*QualificationFrame + 0.08*OpenLoopStrength + 0.05*CloseQuality
  ```

  Hard rewrite if WoundIdentification < 0.6, Specificity < 0.7, AutonomyPreservation < 0.7, or OpenLoopStrength < 0.5. Composition targets: Contrast Ratio ≥ 0.60, Oscillation Frequency ≥ 0.50, Peak Intensity ≥ 8. Open-loop formula: `Unresolved_Tension × Next_Step_Clarity × Low_Pressure_Pull`. [Card: RIG Email Drafting + Reply Protocol: Wound, Mirror, Autonomy, Qualification, Open Loop — ChatGPT 5.5 Prompt](https://doi.org/10.1080/15213260903287259)
- Four reply arcs: (1) Recognition → Tension → Reframe → Choice; (2) Criteria → Tension → Open Loop (“works best for X, not useful for Y”); (3) Autonomy → Loss frame → Identity (“you are free to hold off… waiting is not neutral”); (4) Praise → Filter → Commitment. Diagnose incoming mail with A–F: literal ask, hidden fear, business wound, generic-vendor reply to avoid, felt understanding without diagnosis, tension that must stay unresolved. [Card: RIG OS Master Communications Protocol V1: Unified Email, LinkedIn, Slack, DM, and All Written Communications — Deviation-Calibrated, Prediction-Swarm Validated, 8σ Scoring Engine]
- 20-criterion reply scorecard (targets ≥8–10): wound identified, mirror accuracy, autonomy ≥9, qualification frame, numbers/names, **no generic phrases = 10**, cost of inaction named, did **not** over-explain / diagnose / list features / use exclamation marks, unresolved tension, obvious next step, RPE (exceeded expectation), memory residue, identity, ethical restraint, shorter than they expect. High-scoring close: “If this feels accurate, reply with the part that feels most expensive to leave unchanged.” Median (deviation 0) subject/CTA is “Quick question” / “Hope this finds you well” / “Let me know your thoughts” — treat as fail. [Card: RIG OS Master Communications Protocol V1: Unified Email, LinkedIn, Slack, DM, and All Written Communications — Deviation-Calibrated, Prediction-Swarm Validated, 8σ Scoring Engine]

## Qualification, gatekeeping, website as filter

- **Gatekeeper Method** (reactance / “red button”): shift from “do I want this?” to “am I good enough for this?” Four moves: opening frame → criteria reveal → mirror close → **“but you are free to accept or refuse.”** Carpenter 2013 (42-study meta) is cited on-card as increasing compliance when the autonomy clause is added. After they qualify, “you’d be a great fit” removes “am I good enough” and locks **identity-commitment** (Cialdini consistency): hesitation now contradicts who they just said they are. [Card: This ONE Psychological Trigger Gets Clients to Chase You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VjyDi69NbVc)
- Loss frame is the math: Prospect Theory — losses felt **~2×** equivalent gains, so cost-of-inaction outranks gain copy. Fogg: Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Prompt; spike all three in the same artifact. Access scarcity (exclusivity) beats quantity/time scarcity because it fires reactance **and** social proof. [Card: This ONE Psychological Trigger Gets Clients to Chase You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VjyDi69NbVc)
- Website is a **qualification machine**, not a brochure. Anti-median hard gates: AntiMedianForce < 80, PositioningAuthority < 75, MechanismDemonstration < 75, PeakEndMemory < 70, MotionThesis < 70 → block. Forbidden median pattern: vague hero + “book a call” + testimonial carousel. Required hero: one specific sentence that names a false assumption (example on-card: “Your website is not underperforming because it is ugly. It is underperforming because it makes serious buyers feel like you are optional.”). CTA as threshold, not checkout: Apply for Access / Enter the Decision Room / Diagnose the Pattern. [Card: RIG Website V3 Design + Redesign Protocol: Anti-Median Qualification Machine — Codex 5.5 Prompt](https://doi.org/10.1080/15213260903287259)
- Selling voltage: set a believable expectation, then exceed it in the demo — overpromising **compresses the prediction gap**. Preferred close: “The decision is not whether this is urgent. It is whether you are still willing to let that pattern stay normal.” [Card: RIG OS Behavioral Voltage Doctrine: Dopamine Loading, Desire Architecture, and Product Selling Gates](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1820145116)

## Negotiation and live-call tactics

- **Anchoring:** first number becomes the mental starting line; go first with a bold but believable figure. **Mirroring:** repeat their last few words as a question — they expand, you get information, near-zero risk. **Tactical silence:** after they finish, say nothing; they fill it. **Door-in-the-face:** ask huge, get no, then ask the real thing (reciprocity). **BATNA:** backup if the deal dies; strong BATNA = walk-away power, weak BATNA leaks in tone and concessions. **Ultimatum** only with leverage + a real willingness to walk; otherwise it blows the deal. [Card: Every Negotiation Tactic & How To Use Them Yourself, Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=90svIa07ilQ)
- Carnegie influence that maps to discovery: make the other person feel important **sincerely**; when you need a yes, **do not lead with disagreement** — collect early yeses on what they already believe, then ask. [Card: How to Win Friends and INFLUENCE People (Complete Animated Book Summary)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rvgS9jTGbp4)
