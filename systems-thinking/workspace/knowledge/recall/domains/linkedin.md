## Algorithm and chase mechanics

- LinkedIn is treated as a **cognitive / professional-identity platform** (Layer 3 False Self), not TikTok. Users scan while performing competence. The decision window is **5–15 seconds**: title → image → first line → click / expand / keep scrolling. Distribution is decided in the **first 90 minutes**. Target signals: dwell, qualified comments, saves, thread depth, profile clicks, DMs, second-degree reach — not likes. [Card: LinkedIn Chase Score: Platform-Specific Positioning Math] · [Card: LinkedIn 90-Minute Window Strategy]
- Doctrine numbers (operator estimates, not LinkedIn-official): **dwell ≥ 60 seconds**; **comments weighted 15× likes**; paid LinkedIn CTR floor cited at **~0.38%** (2026 B2B paid-awareness benchmark). Organic engagement-rate benchmarks saved on the viral-topics card: **overall ~5.20%**, **native documents 7.00%**, **multi-image ~6.45%**, **image ~5.30%**, **text ~4.50%**, **link posts ~3.25%**. Implication: if the goal is article reads, the feed object must still behave as a **native LinkedIn artifact** first. [Card: Deviant Doctrine Viral Article Topics] · [Card: LinkedIn Chase Score: Platform-Specific Positioning Math]
- **LinkedIn Positioning Score (LPS)** target **≥ 65/100**:
  `LPS = (0.30 × IntellectualAuthority) + (0.25 × DwellArchitecture) + (0.25 × CommentProvocation) + (0.20 × IdentityDisruption)`
  CP formula: `CP = (C18 × 0.4) + (C15 × 0.3) + (C8 × 0.3)`. Emotional Volatility is **zero-weighted** on LinkedIn (Instagram weapon, not LinkedIn). Novelty Disruption is weighted **0.15**; 2026 “Depth Score” is described as penalizing engagement bait and recycled ideas. [Card: LinkedIn Chase Score: Platform-Specific Positioning Math]
- Conversion path: Scroll → intellectual hook → “See more” → dwell (60+ sec) → identity-performing comment → profile visit → website → qualification. `P(conversion_LI) = LPS × Dwell_Time_Ratio × Comment_Quality × Profile_CTR`. No soft CTA in the close — open loop. [Card: LinkedIn Chase Score: Platform-Specific Positioning Math]
- First two lines are decisive because of truncation. Hook pattern: **Big claim. Short contradiction. Curiosity gap.** Example: “Most local businesses are not losing money because demand is low. / They are losing it after intent appears.” [Card: RIG LinkedIn Article Distribution System v5 — Behavioral Influence, Viral Mechanics, and Hermes Implementation Spec](https://postking.io/blog/linkedin-content-strategy)
