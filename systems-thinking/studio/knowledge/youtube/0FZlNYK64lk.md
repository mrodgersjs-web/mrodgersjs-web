# Granovetter–Strength of Weak Ties (3:09)

Introduces Granovetter's model of social ties as dyadic interpersonal relationships, divided into strong ties (friends and family that supply emotional support) and weak ties (distant or casual connections such as social-media followers that supply new information and opportunities).

## Procedure / models distilled
- Define a social tie as a dyadic interpersonal relationship between two individuals
- Classify ties into strong ties and weak ties
- Identify strong ties with close relationships such as friends and family
- Use strong ties as a source of emotional support
- Identify weak ties with distant or casual relationships
- Use social media followers (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram) as examples of weak ties
- Leverage weak ties to access new information and opportunities
- Follow specific people to curate a stream of incoming information
- Mobilize strong ties to encourage participation in social movements or activism
- Match tie type to function: strong ties for support, weak ties for novel information and opportunities

## Failure modes named
- Expecting emotional support primarily from weak ties rather than strong ties
- Relying only on strong ties and missing the new information and opportunities that weak ties provide
- Treating all social ties as functionally interchangeable

## Studio relevance
02 frame - use the strong/weak tie taxonomy to map which relationships in a system provide emotional support and which channels introduce novel information and opportunities.
