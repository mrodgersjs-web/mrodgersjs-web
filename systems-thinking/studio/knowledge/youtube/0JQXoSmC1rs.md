# Elon Musk first principle reasoning TED

Elon Musk explains that his approach to hard, ambitious problems relies on physics-style first-principles reasoning and aggressively seeking negative feedback.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Use first-principles reasoning: strip a problem down to its fundamental truths and reason upward from there.
- Apply a "physics mindset" to discover truths that are counterintuitive or non-obvious.
- Treat reasoning by analogy as a useful default for daily life, but not as a path to breakthrough innovation.
- Recognize that analogy usually produces only slight variations on what already exists.
- Before designing something new, identify the irreducible facts, constraints, or truths that must hold.
- Synthesize design, technology, and business into one coherent system-level package.
- Let confidence in that integrated package justify taking high-stakes risks repeatedly.
- Actively solicit negative feedback, especially from friends, because most people avoid giving it.
- Treat negative feedback as high-value diagnostic data rather than personal criticism.

## Failure modes named
- Reasoning by analogy when trying to create something genuinely new.
- Failing to seek out or invite negative feedback.
- Dismissing negative feedback once it is offered.
- Relying on habit, common practice, or analogy instead of checking fundamental truths.

02 frame - test problem definitions against first principles and actively seek disconfirming feedback before committing to an analogy-based frame.
