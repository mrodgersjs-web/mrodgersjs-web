# Resilience Engineering 101: Introduction pt 1 - It's all about viability

Resilience engineering is the study of how people, organizations, and technologies sustain viability by adapting to challenges and opportunities under universal constraints.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Treat **viability** as the overarching goal for any adaptive system—biological, social, or technological—because all systems must keep functioning in an evolving world.
- Study adaptation through two core forms: **handling challenge** (extensibility at boundaries) and **seeking opportunity** (growth under constraints).
- Build **extensibility** so performance can stretch at the limits of adaptive range, preventing brittle collapse when challenges hit the boundary.
- Pursue growth and new value while accepting that constraints are inescapable, not temporary inconveniences.
- Hold the constraint that **resources are finite** as a constant; apparent abundance is local and temporary, and finite resources create ubiquitous conflicts.
- Hold the constraint that **change is continuous** as a constant; models, procedures, and technologies therefore become stale and surprise recurs.
- Recognize **other adapting players** at multiple layers of the system; each unit and role is simultaneously handling challenge and seeking opportunity.
- Apply the **law of stretch systems**: any improvement tends to be exploited by stakeholders, raising intensity and tempo until work is pushed back to the edge of the envelope.
- Apply the **law of fluency**: well-adapted activity hides the difficulties and dilemmas it resolves, making hidden risk hard to perceive.
- Center **adaptive capacity** as the key property to engineer; resilience is about how and when systems adapt, not just about preventing failure.

## Failure modes named
- Brittle collapse when a challenge exceeds the current adaptive range.
- Stale models, plans, and technologies due to continuous change, leading to recurring surprise.
- The illusion of unlimited resources, masking the conflicts finite resources create.
- Hidden difficulties and unresolved dilemmas concealed by fluent, well-practiced performance.
- Work being ratcheted to the edge of safe performance by stakeholders exploiting each new improvement.
