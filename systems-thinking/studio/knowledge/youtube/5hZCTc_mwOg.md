# How to Get SO Many Customers with AI it feels ILLEGAL

Map AI to the five phases of sales—prospecting, qualifying, presenting, objection handling, and closing/delivery—while keeping humans in the loop for taste, vision, and trust.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Use the "five phases of sales" as the system map: prospecting → qualifying → presenting → objection handling → closing/delivery.
- Automate prospecting with AI-powered CRM/lead tools by feeding them your current customer examples and letting them build an ICP-based lead list with contact details and social profiles.
- Apply the 10-80-10 rule: 10% ideation, 80% AI execution, 10% human review/feedback, so you only intervene where taste and judgment matter.
- Pre-qualify leads with an AI voice/chat agent before they can book a sales call, so your calendar fills only with high-intent prospects.
- Build a "sales offer" ChatGPT project with your offer template, product overview, and each prospect's CRM data and transcripts; create a new chat per buyer to generate a custom proposal and call script.
- Deliver proposals human-to-human on a scheduled review call rather than sending them by email, so you can surface concerns and ask for commitment live.
- Use AI to generate VSSL-style copy that agitates the buyer's pain and states the value proposition in language sharper than the buyer's own.
- Treat objections as obstacles to be surfaced early; use AI to generate questions and talk tracks that help the buyer see the value and make the leap themselves.
- Transcribe sales calls or chat logs, upload at least five into an AI, and ask for missed insights and ways to improve future calls.
- Roleplay objection handling with an AI instructed to act as a world-class salesperson in your specific industry, then request a score/grade at the end.
- Reframe "closing" as "enrollment" and design immediate post-purchase rituals: ask "How do you feel?", deliver a quick win fast, and build in win-sharing agreements for momentum and testimonials.
- Let AI handle backend onboarding busywork so you can stay in the celebratory, relationship-building layer with new customers.
- Preserve your humanity as the differentiator: taste, vision, care, and real conversation cannot be replaced by AI.

## Failure modes named
- Treating every lead as a potential customer instead of filtering for actual buyers.
- Celebrating call volume as a success metric when most calls are with unqualified prospects.
- Sending proposals over email and losing the chance to address objections and secure commitment in real time.
- Waiting for the prospect to voice an objection instead of proactively naming and resolving it.
- Using AI to convince people rather than to give them courage to take a step they already want to take.
- Trying to remove humans from sales instead of keeping humans in the loop where trust, empathy, and taste matter.
