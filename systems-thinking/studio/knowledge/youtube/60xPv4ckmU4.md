# Transfer of Learning| Types, theories and Implications

This video defines transfer of learning as applying knowledge, skills, or attitudes from one situation to another, then presents its three main types, three classical theories, and five educational implications.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Define transfer of learning as the carryover of habits of thinking, feeling, working, knowledge, or skills from one learning area to another.
- Recognize positive transfer: prior learning in one situation facilitates learning in a new situation.
- Recognize negative transfer: prior learning in one situation makes learning in another situation harder.
- Recognize zero or neutral transfer: prior learning has no effect on the new learning task.
- Apply the theory of generalization by helping learners extract broad principles from one task so those principles can apply to related tasks.
- Apply the theory of identical elements by increasing the number of similar or identical elements between an old task and a new task to boost transfer.
- Apply the Gestalt/configuration theory by designing situations where learners perceive structural similarities between the old and new contexts.
- Develop ideals and attitudes in learners so they carry over across life contexts.
- Encourage critical thinking and understanding of principles rather than rote memorization.
- Explain the purpose, meaning, and real-life application of subject matter to learners.
- Link new material explicitly to what learners have already learned.
- Relate topics within one subject to topics in other subjects and to real-world situations.

## Failure modes named
- Negative transfer: prior language or skill learning interferes with new learning.
- Rote memorization without principle understanding reduces the chance of transfer.
- Teaching subjects as isolated domains prevents learners from seeing connections.
- Failing to systematize and relate knowledge to multiple situations limits its applicability.
- Missing structural similarities between contexts blocks configuration-based transfer.
