# The Toyota Production System as Taught by Toyota - Episode 05

This episode shows how a physical kanban card is converted into a replenishment request that pulls parts from supplier plants to Toyota’s assembly line in the quantity needed, only when needed.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Deliver parts to assembly in the quantities needed and only when they are needed.
- Use electric carts to move unloaded parts from delivery trucks to racks alongside each assembly line.
- Have the line-side worker remove the kanban card from the parts box as soon as the first part is used.
- Collect removed kanban cards regularly and place them in a kanban post at each process.
- Scan or enter the kanban cards into a computer system to generate replenishment requests.
- Send replenishment requests to parts manufacturers with the required quantities and timing.
- Print supplier-side kanban cards for each truck shipment.
- Have shipping personnel collect the parts boxes that correspond to each kanban card.
- Produce the corresponding parts at the supplier plant as indicated on the kanban.
- Consolidate shipments until a full truckload is gathered, then truck the parts to the vehicle assembly plant.
- Treat each kanban as a pull signal triggered by actual consumption at the line.
- Use kanban to link Toyota plants with supplier plants and to link processes inside Toyota plants, enabling just-in-time manufacturing.
