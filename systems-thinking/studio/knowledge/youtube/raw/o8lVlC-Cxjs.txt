Companies like Apple often group their
teams by department, marketing, finance,
engineering, etc. Each team focuses on
its specialty, helping maintain high
levels of expertise. The divisional
structure used by companies like Amazon.
Amazon has divisions for its retail
business, Amazon Web Services, AWS, and
its consumer electronics. Each division
operates semi-independently with its own
teams, making them more flexible and
focused on specific markets or products.
The challenge, it can create overlap in
resources across divisions. Apple
originally used a functional structure
organizing by departments. Each unit
focused on its area, perfect for
innovation and tight control. Large
companies like Amazon or later Apple use
a divisional structure. Each division
runs almost like a mini company based on
products or regions. Functional works
well for startups and focus. Divisional
