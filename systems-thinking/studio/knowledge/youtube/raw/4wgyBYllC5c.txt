Welcome back to our video series
where we walk you through Funnel
to provide a better understanding of how
to set up and optimize your subscription.
In this video,
we’ll walk you through using Funnel’s Activate feature
to combat signal loss
and improve your marketing performance
by double digits.
You’ve imported and harmonized your data,
and you’ve created reports for your team,
your clients, and your executives.
Now what?
The digital landscape is changing.
With the decline of third-party cookies
and increasing privacy restrictions,
ad platforms are flying blind.
Because of this,
your internal data like CRM data
or offline conversions
is the most accurate signal.
Using Activate helps optimize
your campaigns to ensure your budget
is being spent on the customers
that actually drive revenue,
not just website clicks.
Using a Conversion API,
or CAPI, is an easy way
to send first-party conversion data
back to your ad platforms.
By sending this data
back to ad platforms,
you’re helping those algorithms learn
what makes your campaigns successful,
resulting in an uplift in performance.
This means more conversions
and lower cost per action.
Creating a conversion upload
in Funnel is easy.
All you need is your conversion data
from your source of truth.
That could be events you’ve been tracking
on your website, conversions in your CRM,
or wherever you store
the signals that matter to your business.
If you haven’t connected that data,
head over to Connect section
in the left menu
and add your data sources.
Once your data is in,
the hard part is done.
Then go to Activate
in the left menu, choose
Create a Conversion Upload and select
the platform you want to send data to.
Most platforms require that you set up
the conversion action on their side first.
Make sure that’s in place,
then just pick your credentials
and choose the conversion action
you want to send data to.
Think of it as selecting which “bucket”
the new conversions should land in
so the platform can optimize
using the fresh data.
During the field mapping step,
you’ll build the table you want to send.
Select the fields
you already have in Funnel.
You’ll always need
at least one user identifier.
That’s what lets the platform
match your conversions to real users
and gives you stronger attribution.
Then select the conversion time
and conversion value.
These can be platform-native
fields or custom fields
you’ve already created in Funnel.
In advanced settings,
we apply smart defaults,
but you can customize the fields
to fit your needs.
For example, choose your scheduling cadence
so your uploads align
with your operational and reporting needs.
After that, you can preview your data
to make sure everything looks right
before creating
and uploading your conversions.
Your data will be on its way
to the platform in no time.
Come back to the details page anytime
you want to see what’s happening
with your upload.
Now you can sit back and start getting
the benefits of Activate,
like improved ROAS, better attribution,
and higher-quality optimization signals.
