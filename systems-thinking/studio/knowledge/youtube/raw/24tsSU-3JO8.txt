Recent research in cognitive science has
begun to validate what polymaths have
always known intuitively. Diverse
knowledge creates synergistic effects
that are greater than the sum of their
parts. The medi effect. France Johansson
coined the term medi effect to describe
what happens when ideas from different
fields intersect. He shows how
breakthrough innovations consistently
occur at the boundaries between
disciplines where polymathic thinkers
can see connections that specialists
miss. The key insight is that innovation
rates increase exponentially as you
combine insights from more fields. It's
not just additive, it's multiplicative.

cognitive flexibility. Research
researchers have found that people with
diverse knowledge show enhanced
cognitive flexibility, the ability to
switch between different conceptual
