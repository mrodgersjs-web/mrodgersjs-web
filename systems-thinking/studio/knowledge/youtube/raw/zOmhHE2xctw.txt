there is no net flow.
For every ion that moves in one direction,
another one moves in the opposite direction.
When the membrane voltage differs from this equilibrium potential,
there is a net flow of ions
that tends to drive the membrane potential towards the equilibrium.
Let's make this more concrete with an example.
In a typical neuron,
there are about 30 times as many potassium ions inside the cell as outside,

If the membrane potential is less negative,

the electrical force is too weak &amp; the diffusion dominates,

Conversely, if the membrane voltage is more negative than the equilibrium potential,
