in this podcast we will discuss the
importance of deliberate practice and
the essential components that go into
this process of improvement
deliberate practice is not a practice
exclusively found in education
all professions have built-in processes
and opportunities for employees to
improve on their essential job tasks
take for example a musician
for a musician to perfect their craft in
playing an instrument they must
deliberately practice notes scales and
songs to be able to perform
the average orchestra member has
deliberately practiced over ten thousand
hours to be able to play at this level
the concept of deliberate practice
revolves around setting specific
conditions in a predetermined growth
area proficiency improves with time
spent on deliberate and focused practice
deliberate practice contains three
essential components
clear and focused tasks clear criteria
for success and the motivation to engage
in deliberate practice
in order for deliberate practice tasks
to be clear and focused they must be
consistent with the teachers skill and
knowledge level
being honest and focused on this aspect
will allow for true growth to occur
taking the self-assessment and
reflecting on past practice can help
with understanding current skill and
once clear and focused tasks have been
articulated feedback on the area can be
accurately given and responded to
deliberate practice should always
include a clear criteria for success
specific tasks and activities should be
established and practiced and specific
feedback should be given
when thinking about criteria for success
the ultimate data to be considered
should be student learning using pre and
post test data can provide valuable data
for measuring growth
within deliberate practice focus areas
the final component for deliberate
practice is the motivation to engage in
the process
in order for our students to be truly
college career and life ready we all
must continue to work on our own skills
as educators
reasonable growth targets should always
be considered so like our students we
can see success in our work
