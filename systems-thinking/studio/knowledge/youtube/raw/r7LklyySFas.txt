the batna negotiation method which
stands for best alternative to a
negotiated agreement was popularized in
the 1980s by researchers Roger Fisher
and William Yuri of Harvard University's
negotiation program
this technique allows you to plan a
fallback solution in order to reach a
favorable agreement during a negotiation
for both the seller and the buyer
although the batnam method is often used
in commercial negotiation it is also
useful in all kinds of negotiation
contexts
this technique is important for several
reasons
it offers negotiating power
it offers an alternative if negotiations
fail
it determines the last acceptable
position for each party

second
