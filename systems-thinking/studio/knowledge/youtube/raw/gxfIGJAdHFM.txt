Hey guys, in this video, I'm going to
show you exactly how to set up an
inactive page on a deactivated sales
funnel. This is super important if
you've previously shared your funnel
link on social media or in emails. When
an offer ends and you turn off the
funnel, you don't want to lose all that
traffic clicking on your old links.
Instead, you can show visitors a custom
message and redirect them to a new
active page or a wait list.
To do this, you will need a deactivated
sales funnel and the URL of an active
opt-in page. Let's dive in. Navigate to
the sites tab in the main menu and click
on sales funnels. Next, click on the
drop-down menu. You will see two
options: active and inactive. Select
inactive and press enter. Now, click on
the title of your deactivated sales
funnel. At the very bottom of the funnel
steps list on the left, click on
inactive page to open the editor. Here,
you can fully customize the page. We
highly recommend adding a short message
letting visitors know the offer has
ended along with a button inviting them
to subscribe to your newsletter or get
notified about future updates. To make
the button work, click on it to open its
settings in the left panel. Change the
button action to open URL. Then, simply
paste the exact URL of your active
opt-in page into the URL box and save
your changes.
Now, whenever someone visits any link
associated with this deactivated funnel,
this inactive page will automatically be
displayed. One very important note
before we finish.
Because this funnel is deactivated, it
cannot collect leads directly. Even if
you add an email input field to this
page and set the button to submit form,
it will not work. You must use a button
to redirect users to a page that is
currently active. And that's it. You now
know how to easily catch and redirect
lost traffic using an inactive page.
