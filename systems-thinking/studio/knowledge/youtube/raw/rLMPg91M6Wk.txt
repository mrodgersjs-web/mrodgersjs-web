Welcome to my YouTube channel Nexus IT
Insights. Today we are going to talk
about a very important concept in the
world of technology. Site reliability
engineering, also known as S sur. What
is site reliability engineering? Site
reliability engineering is a discipline
that combines software engineering with
IT operations. Its main purpose is to
make sure that systems, applications,
and services are reliable, available,
and scalable. In simple words, S sur is
all about keeping websites and
applications running smoothly even when
millions of people are using them at the
same time. Why was S sur created? The
idea of S sur started at Google.
Traditional system administrators used
to manage servers and fix issues
manually. But as systems grew larger,
manual work was no longer enough. Google
introduced site reliability engineering
so that engineers could use coding and
automation to solve operational
problems. This approach made their
systems more stable and efficient. Core
principles of S sur. There are a few key
principles in site reliability
engineering. Reliability first. The main
goal of S sur is to ensure that systems
are always available and working as
expected. Automation over manual work.
Instead of fixing problems manually,
SRRES write scripts and build tools to
automate tasks. SLI, SLOs's and SLAs.
These are metrics in targets that
measure system performance and
reliability. For example, how much
uptime a service should have. Air
budgets. This is a way to balance
reliability with innovation. It defines
how much downtime is acceptable so that
teams can release new features without
affecting stability. What do SRRES
actually do? S SRRES have a wide range
of responsibilities such as monitoring
systems and services to detect problems
early, managing incidents and outages
when they happen, writing code to
automate repetitive operational tasks,
improving system scalability and
performance, and working closely with
developers to make sure new features are
reliable before release. In short, they
act as the bridge between developers and
operations teams. Why is S sur import
important in today's world? Every second
of downtime can cause big financial
losses and damage customer trust. For
example, if a streaming service or an
online store goes down, even for a short
time, millions of users are affected.
Site reliability engineering makes sure
that systems can handle failures,
recover quickly, and continue to provide
a smooth experience to users. That's why
S sur is considered a critical part of
modern technology companies. So to
summarize, site reliability engineering
is a practice that uses software
engineering to make systems reliable,
scalable, and efficient. It ensures that
the applications we use everyday remain
available without interruptions.
