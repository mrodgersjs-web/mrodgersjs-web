5-second formula breakdown. Add this in
cutaways. Make each formula feel like a
powerup, not punishment. Example:
kinematic equation v= u plus a t.
Translation. Your final speed equals

time. It's literally how fast you get
when you keep speeding up. That's it.
Every time you break it down like this,

just a calculation. That mindset makes
solving them way more fun and fast.
