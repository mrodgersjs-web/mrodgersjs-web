Hello everyone and welcome to
introduction to agentbased modeling on
complexity explorer. In this course you
will learn about agent based
simulations, complex systems and how to
solve some fun problems such as how
birds flock, how ants for age for food,
how some neighborhoods end up culturally
segregated or how you can create
artificial forms of life on your
computer. You will learn how lots of
macro phenomena and patterns we observe
in our daily lives emerge from very
simple micro rules of behavior. For this
course, no prior programming experience
is necessary as we will help you build
your skills from the ground up both in
computer programming and in conceptually
understanding the computational rules
underlying some real life patterns. You
will first start with how to build an
agentbased model or ABM from the ground
up. the components of an ABM as well as
the history of ABMs. You will get
familiar with some classic models such
as sugarcape or voids and by the end of
the nine weeks of the course, you will
become proficient in using advanced
modeling techniques through geographical
or network type of extensions. You will
build your own models in the topic that
interests you the most. I hope you will
enjoy this course and have a lot of fun
while learning some very useful skills
and methods.
