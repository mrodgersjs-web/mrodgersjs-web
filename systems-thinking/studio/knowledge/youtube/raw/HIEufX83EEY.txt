let us introduce the production system
based on the just in time Concept in
which we produce only what is needed
when it's needed and in the amount
needed
before implementing just in time we
applied the push system where parts were
delivered from the previous process to
the next regardless of what was actually
with the just in time method we now use
the pull system where parts are pulled
from the previous process only in
quantities needed for the next process
first cards called kanban are attached
to the part or parts box workers remove
the kanban cards when using the part or
the first piece in the Box
they then go to the preceding process
with the kanban to retrieve the
corresponding part or parts box
when the parts are retrieved a kanban
for those parts is removed and sent to
the previous process with production
instructions
this is the basic concept of just in
time
