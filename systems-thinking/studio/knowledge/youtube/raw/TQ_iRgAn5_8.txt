what is a batna
if you have ever read the landmark book
getting to yes or any other negotiation
book one of the first things you will
learn about is batna your batna is
critical to a favorable outcome for
negotiating anything
batna is an acronym for best alternative
to a negotiated agreement
what will you purchase or do if you
don't agree to the deal on the table for
example
let's say i'm negotiating the price for
a used car
the owner is firm at a thousand dollars
i like this car but the high mileage
makes me think that it's only worth 800.
i know that there is another car that i
like almost as much down the street for
8.50
that other car is my batna
your best alternative to a negotiated
agreement will be an option that you
like equally as well so as to eliminate
any cost of walking away and buying
something else
this is basically a measurement of your
power to walk away from their current
offer
your batna will be influenced by many
features some that are tangible such as
color condition aesthetics and quality
and some that are intangible such as
convenience speed and time of
acquisition and circumstances
this is why ballparks can get away with
charging three dollars for a 50 cent can
of coke
your baton at that point is suffering
for three hours until you get home
those who know their batna negotiate far
more effectively than those who don't
how to improve your batna
the best way to improve your batna is to
have good market knowledge or to be
negotiating with several sellers at the
same time
you need to know what is out there and
the acquisition costs associated with
each option
for example if i am in the market for a
specific make and model for a new car i
will call three or four dealerships and
get information regarding price
availability options and financing if
needed
that way when i talk to a salesperson i
can leverage my batna into creating
price concessions or value-added
services not yet provided
knowing your batna is essential and will
determine your satisfaction level
resulting from any negotiation
