What is transfer of learning?
Transfer of learning happens when a
person recognizes a situation that is
related to what he experienced before.
And this experience allows them to apply
or use what they know in this new
situation.
Here are the four transfers of learning.
Positive transfer, negative transfer,
near transfer, and far transfer.
First one is positive transfer.
We learn things that are related to each
other.
When you try to learn something from one
context, you'll be able to learn another
task that is related to it. For example,
a musician who knows how to play the
violin will easily know how to play a
piano.
A speaker of Spanish may find it easy to
speak the Mexican language.
Second is negative transfer.
This is the exact opposite of positive
transfer.
This means that learning something from
another context may hinder that person
to perform another.
This creates a negative effect in doing
another task. For instance, a Visayan
speaker may find it difficult to speak
in English smoothly because of the
different sounds of vowels.
This causes a Visayan speaker to
pronounce English words in an awkward
way.
Third is near transfer.
This is also known as specific transfer.
In this transfer, the students are able
to answer the questions since these are
very similar or nearly the same with
what they know.
For instance, the students are able to
answer the major examination since the
questions are similar to their seatwork
activities. Or the student is able to
operate an iPhone well since she had a
phone before but an older model.
The last one is far transfer.
This is also known as general transfer.
This is commonly used if we try to apply
what we learned from a very different
context.
For instance, there are lessons from
different fables that we can apply. In
the fable, the goose that lays golden
eggs, we can apply its lesson in
corporate fields.
You may try to say that in order for us
to have a successful business, we must
take care of our employees.
Clearly, these are two different or far
contexts, but you were able to connect
them.
