this technique is called the premortem
and you should think of it as like a
postmortem before anything's actually
died it comes from Gary Klein the author
of The Power of intuition with a
premortem what you do is you imagine
that you've carried out the idea you're
talking about 12 months have passed and
it's been a disaster total failure what
you do is you ask everyone in the group
to individually and silently write down
what went wrong and ask them to go for
lots of reasons as many things as they
can that may have caused this to be a
disaster after maybe 10 minutes of
Silent working create a matrix of
problems looking for the ones which are
the most serious and the most likely
you'll find that lots of people have
come up with similar problems so what
you're looking for now are the ones that
pose the most serious risk to your
project success and the ones which are
most likely to happen ask yourself which
of these problems are both serious and
likely and in our control and what do we
need to do now to avoid them from
happening
and the second area you look at is which
of these problems are serious and likely
but out of our control in which case you
need to make a contingency plan what do
we do if this
happens you'll find that a premortem is
great for puncturing optimism and group
think where people are just saying yes
because they're hoping the idea will
work you'll find more techniques like
this on my website or by following me on
Twitter
