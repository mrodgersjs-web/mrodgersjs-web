So you want to know what Conway's Law is?
Okay.
In 1973, actor/comedian Tim Conway overthrew a small island nation and declared martial
law or, as it became known there, Conway's Law.
Actually, Conway's Law is all about communication.
Basically it states that the way that we design and build products reflects the structure
of communication among the people building them.
Let's just say that you're part of a team building a software app.
If your team is separated into different departments that don't communicate very much, chances
are the end product will reflect those same gaps in communication between, say, the front-end
application and the back-end user code.
And that's because individuals amongst a team tend to optimize their work based on their
own unique skills and expertise.
So it's so important to get individuals in different teams to work together as a team,
to collaborate together.
And that way the end product will reflect that same unified communication structure.
