one way to be well prepared for
negotiation is having a strong batner
batner stands for best alternative to a
negotiated agreement it is your backup
plan if you can't make the deal you want
it is your walk away point the benefits
of using batner it provides an
alternative if negotiations fall through
it provides negotiating power it
determines your reservation your walk
away point and it gives you confidence
in negotiations batner should always be
considered before a negotiation takes
place it is never wise to enter into a
negotiation without knowing your batner
let's take a closer look at the batner
model and how each party can best
negotiate on an agreement
Zopa stands for zone of possible
agreement and is the overlap between the
buyer and sellers settlement range the
seller's settlement range is a bidable
range acceptable to the seller and the
buyer's settlement range is a bidable
range acceptable to the buyer the buyer
and seller's worst case is the
reservation point of the respective
parties
for example if the buyer offers a price
that is lower than the seller's worst
case scenario then the seller may have
to find another buyer unless compromise
is possible and if the seller offers a
price that is higher than the buyer's
worst case then the buyer is better off
with an alternative solution unless a
compromise can be made
let's look at a real life example Jane
wants a car she wants to pay 500 pounds
but can go as high as 750 pounds for the
right car Tony wants to sell a car for 1
000 pounds but is willing to take 650
pounds as the lowest offer if the buyer
pays cash
this means the Zopa range is between 650
pounds to 750 pounds which is the point
where Jane and Tony could agree if they
negotiate well
being prepared doing your research to
determine batner means you are not
forced into accepting a bad deal which
you could be if you go into a
negotiation unprepared not knowing your
batner
