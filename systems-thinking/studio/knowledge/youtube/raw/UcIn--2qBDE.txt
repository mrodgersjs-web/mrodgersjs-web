Hey guys, in this video you will learn
how to create a deadline for a
salesunnel in System I.O. The deadline
feature allows you to offer a special
deal for a limited time, giving each
prospect a specific access period based
on when they registered. This feature
can be used on order forms and sales
pages. To get started, navigate to your
salesunnel and click on the deadline
settings button near the top right. The
deadline settings page will open. Toggle
the switch to enable deadline feature.
Next, you need to configure the time
limit in the deadline page expires in
field. Specify the number of days the
offer will be available. For example, if
you set it to 6 days, the page will
expire for that specific user 6 days
after they register. You also have the
option to set a specific expiration time
by checking the box time when deadline
page should expire and entering the
exact time. Once the deadline passes,
you need to decide where to send the
user. In the field labeled funnel step
to redirect to once the deadline has
expired, select the page you want them
to see, such as an offerended page.
Finally, confirm which pages this
deadline applies to by checking the
boxes under available funnel steps for
deadline funnel. Click save to activate
the settings. If you want to display
this deadline to your visitors, you can
easily sync a countdown timer with these
settings. Open your page editor. Locate
the countdown element in the sidebar and
drag and drop it onto your page. Then
click on the countdown timer element you
just added to open its settings. For the
countdown type, select same as deadline
funnel settings. And there you go. This
is how you create a deadline for a
salesunnel using system IO.
