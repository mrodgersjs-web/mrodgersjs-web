Hanoi, 1902.
The French control the city and they

rule running under all of it. In 1975, a

cannot stop seeing it. A sociologist

twin of this rule back in 1976. The more

Pay schools for test scores, and you

None of these people wake up as

So, look for a second at the numbers

The sales quota. The GPA. The follower

The little ring on your watch counting

Every one of them began as a humble
stand-in, a proxy for something real you

And the moment it became the target, it
started, slowly, to peel away from the
thing it was supposed to represent.
The measure was never the goal.
It was only ever a finger pointing at
it.
And the harder you squeeze the finger,
