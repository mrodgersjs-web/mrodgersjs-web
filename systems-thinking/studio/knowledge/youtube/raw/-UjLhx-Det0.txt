Hey guys, in this video you will learn
how to activate a salesunnel in system
IO. Activating a funnel allows you to
restore a previously deactivated funnel
and make it accessible again. To get
started, navigate to the sites tab in
the menu and click on sales funnels. By
default, only active funnels are shown,
so you need to change the filter. Click
on the drop-own menu next to the search
bar and select inactive. Then click on
the filter button to display the list of
all your inactive funnels. Locate the
specific funnel you want to reactivate.
Once you find it, click on the three
dots located on the right side of that
funnels row. From the menu options that
appear, click on activate. A popup
window will appear asking you to confirm
the action. Simply click the confirm
button. You will see a notification
message at the top of the screen
confirming that the funnel has been
successfully activated. And there you
go. This is how you activate a funnel
using system IO.
