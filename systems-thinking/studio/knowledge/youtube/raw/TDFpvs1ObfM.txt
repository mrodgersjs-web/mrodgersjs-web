In this video I will
show you an example of so-called swarm intelligence.
Swarm intelligence is a
phenomenon that can appear when a large
group of simple individuals interacts
with each other and its surroundings and
consistently applies a set of simple rules.
This leads to complex behavior of
the group and gives it amazing
problem-solving capabilities.
The intelligence demonstrated by the group
goes far beyond that of a single individual.
The emerging intelligence in
a group of animals is similar to the
intelligence of the human brain which in
fact is also the result of a large group
of interacting individual neuron cells.
Ant colonies are well known for their
ability of complex problem solving.
Some people even regard an ant colony as one
single organism consisting of individual
ants instead of cells.
In this video I show you a very special ant nest.
This colony consists of thousands of tiny individuals.
I have been studying it intensely for more than 10 years.
I have developed a method to train this super organism in problem solving and
performing other complicated tasks.
Year after year, day after day, every
successful performance by the ants was
rewarded with extra food
and failures were punished.
As the number of individuals is growing exponentially the performance improved amazingly.
The number of escaping attempts has
increased dramatically while their
tactics become smarter and smarter.
At the moment the brainpower of the colony
is absolutely amazing.
This video shows that this special ant colony is even
able to send messages to the outside world...
