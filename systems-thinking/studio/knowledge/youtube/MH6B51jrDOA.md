# Claude Just Gave Me 1,000s More Backlinks (Here's How)

Use a Claude prompt to discover high-intent, citation-heavy search queries your business should appear for, then manually or automatically turn those into backlinks and AI-overview visibility.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Run a Claude prompt that analyzes your brand and outputs ~15 high buying-intent search prompts that are likely to generate citations and backlinks.
- Switch Claude to "Sonic" mode to get faster, lower-token output when deep reasoning is unnecessary.
- Copy the generated high-intent prompts and search them in Claude, ChatGPT, or Google to see which pages and domains currently rank or are cited.
- Scroll past AI overviews and organic results to identify the exact sites being cited, treating each citation source as a potential backlink target.
- Vet each opportunity by checking site quality/trust, whether it accepts guest posts or article submissions, and whether it requires payment based on its domain rating (DR).
- Reach out to relevant sites for article submissions, features, or paid placements to earn backlinks from existing high-intent result pages.
- Publish dedicated SEO articles targeting each high-intent prompt so your own business can rank and appear in AI overviews.
- Weave explicit brand assets into each article—calls to action, internal links, sitemap references, and your knowledge base—so AI systems associate the content with your business.
- Ensure service/money pages also mention the same prompts and terms to reinforce topical authority across both articles and commercial pages.
- Use an automated SEO writing tool (Arvo) to generate articles with meta descriptions, H2s, H3s, and alt text optimized for AI citations and overviews.
- Submit your business name, URL, and sitemap to Arvo's backlink exchange to receive automated backlinks from other exchange participants' articles.
- Combine manual outreach and the automated backlink exchange to accelerate total backlink growth.

## Failure modes named
- Targeting low buying-intent queries that won't deliver qualified traffic or valuable citations.
- Wasting tokens by using deep-thinking mode when a faster model setting is sufficient for research outputs.
- Assuming every citation source is a good backlink opportunity without checking trust, submission policies, or cost.
- Treating local businesses as equivalent to broad publishers; local service niches can be harder because high-intent results are closer to a purchase decision and have fewer guest-post targets.
- Relying only on manual outreach and ignoring automated channels, slowing backlink velocity.
- Building backlinks only from low-DR or spammy sites instead of prioritizing higher-DR, trustworthy domains.
