# How To Identify Trends in Markets (Never Guess Again)

The video teaches three SuperTrend-based confirmation strategies to distinguish genuine trends from sideways chop and defines simple exit rules for each setup.

## Procedure / models distilled
- The SuperTrend indicator combines momentum tracking with ATR-based volatility; by itself, green means buy/long and red means sell/short.
- Strategy 1 setup: add three SuperTrend indicators to the chart and set ATR period / multiplier pairs to 12/3, 10/1, and 11/2, then turn off the long/short icons.
- Strategy 1 entry filter: only go long when all three SuperTrend lines turn green, and only go short when all three turn red; mixed colors mean sideways/low-momentum conditions, so stay out.
- Strategy 1 exit options: close at a 1.5 risk/reward take-profit, or exit as soon as any one of the three lines flips color.
- Strategy 2 setup: use one SuperTrend with default settings plus a 200-period Exponential Moving Average to align short-term and long-term trend direction.
- Strategy 2 long rule: SuperTrend green and price above the 200 EMA; short rule: SuperTrend red and price below the 200 EMA; wait for both conditions before entering.
- Strategy 2 exit options: close at a 1.5 risk/reward take-profit, or exit when the SuperTrend line changes color.
- Strategy 3 setup: add the Ichimoku indicator, disable the top three default plots, and color the Kumo cloud background purple to keep the chart readable.
- Strategy 3 long rule: SuperTrend green and price above the Kumo cloud; do not enter if price is inside or below the cloud.
- Strategy 3 short rule: SuperTrend red and price below the Kumo cloud; wait for both conditions before shorting.
- Strategy 3 exit options: close at a 1.5 risk/reward take-profit, or exit when price re-enters the Kumo cloud.
- Treat SuperTrend as a directional signal only and require confirmation from a higher-timeframe or structural filter before committing capital.

## Failure modes named
- Using SuperTrend alone in sideways, low-momentum markets produces frequent false buy/sell signals and losing trades.
- Taking every green or red SuperTrend signal without a confirmation filter chops the portfolio.
- Entering before all three SuperTrend lines agree assumes momentum that is not actually present.
- Entering long while price is still below the 200 EMA or Kumo cloud ignores the higher-timeframe trend.
- Shorting while price is still above the 200 EMA or Kumo cloud fights the prevailing structural trend.
- Trading without a defined exit rule leaves profits and losses unmanaged.
