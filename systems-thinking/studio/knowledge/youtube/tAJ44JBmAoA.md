# How To Find Product-Market-Fit For Your Business | Alex Hormozi Mastermind LIVE Q&A Pt.7

Hormozi reframes product-market fit as audience-market fit first: before changing the product, verify the offer is being shown to people who would actually benefit from it.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Test product-market fit by asking people to join—the "program special" method of direct invitation and conversion is the fastest signal.
- Segment by who is seeing the offer, not just what the offer is; audience mismatch can disguise itself as product failure.
- Define the ideal participant profile for your community or offer in concrete terms (e.g., "new bowlers averaging 200 in a local league").
- Diagnose the direction of misfit: is the market wrong, the message wrong, or the product wrong?
- Treat content as a filter for the market; content that appeals broadly may attract people who will never buy.
- Align product and audience bidirectionally: adjust the product to attract the right people, or adjust the audience targeting to fit the existing product.
- Measure resonance by whether the core economic buyer gets immediate value, not by general-interest engagement.
- Optimize for the segment whose primary goal your offer directly serves, rather than adjacent-interest browsers.

## Failure modes named
- Running the right offer in front of the wrong market (e.g., local dog grooming ads shown to prospects in another city).
- Assuming the product is broken when the real problem is insufficient or incorrect reach.
- Using wide, general-interest content that dilutes the audience and lowers perceived relevance to buyers.
- Creating content one or two steps removed from the core buyer's primary motivation (e.g., lifestyle content for people who joined to make money).
- Attracting people who appreciate the content but are not the addressable market for the offer.
