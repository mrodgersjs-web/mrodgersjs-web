# 25 Years of Sales Knowledge in 34 Minutes

Daniel Priestley lays out a complete revenue operating system called LAPS (leads, appointments, presentations, sales) and shows how to move strangers from attention to money-in-the-bank through content, structured sales conversations, disciplined follow-up, and AI tooling.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Run the LAPS operating rhythm: generate **Leads → Appointments → Presentations → Sales** every week as the core engine of the business.
- Generate warm leads by moving prospects through a three-stage funnel: **short-form content → long-form content → signal of interest**.
- Post short-form content daily on social media because prospects need roughly **11 exposures within 90 days** before they notice you for the first time.
- Build up to **four pieces of long-form content** over 12 months so prospects can spend 2-7 hours with you and develop trust.
- Convert interest into leads using landing-page signals of interest: waiting lists, forms, assessments, discussion groups, or live webinars.
- Follow up new leads immediately with one goal only: **book an appointment**, not close the sale.
- Book appointments using a four-step outreach flow: **reaffirm relevance → ask permission (live calls) → deliver a hook pitch → ask for a diary commitment**.
- Use the **Name-Same-Fame-Pain-Aim-Game** hook pitch to explain who you are, what category you fit, why you stand out, the pain you solve, your aim, and your bigger vision in 30-45 seconds.
- Treat the sales presentation as a 10-step process that ends with **completing**: either onboard the client now or schedule a concrete next appointment in the diary.
- Exploit the **48-hour peak** after a presentation and plan at least **seven follow-up contacts** before cooling a prospect.
- Keep follow-up relevant by referencing presentation-derived reasons, new insights, customer testimonials, special offers, and timed check-ins.
- Move stalled prospects into a **nurture sequence** of friendly social/email touch points, then escalate to a **reactivation campaign** using a “Have you given up on X?” email plus an assessment or calculator.
- Use AI to transcribe sales presentations, extract common themes between calls that won and calls that bombed, and refine your offer and method.
- Follow up with speed: contact leads within minutes, not days, and avoid “playing it cool” or being aloof.
- Build confidence and predictability by maintaining a **consistent weekly LAPS rhythm** rather than stop-start bursts; track a stable dashboard of leads, appointments, presentations, and sales.

## Failure modes named
- Skipping ahead to presentations and sales before expanding lead flow.
- Leaving money on the table by not posting every day on social media.
- Letting the 90-day attention clock reset before prospects notice you 11 times.
- Avoiding follow-up because it feels “icky,” exhausting, or frustrating.
- Playing it cool or delaying contact as if sales were dating.
- Failing to document follow-up reasons during the presentation and losing hot prospects.
- Giving up after one or two follow-ups instead of reaching seven touches.
- Stop-starting sales activity instead of maintaining a steady weekly LAPS cadence.
- Running the pipeline without a dashboard, making revenue unpredictable.
