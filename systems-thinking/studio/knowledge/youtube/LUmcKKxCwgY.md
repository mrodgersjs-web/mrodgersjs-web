# Personality Lecture 16: Eysenck, Gray, & DeYoung / Traits & Biology (Spring 2024)

A lecture tracing biological personality theories from Hippocrates' four humors through Eysenck's arousal-based PEN model, Gray's reinforcement-sensitivity theory, and DeYoung's personality neuroscience linking Big Five traits to neurotransmitter systems and brain structures.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Map ancient four-humors temperaments onto two modern trait dimensions: extraversion and emotional stability/neuroticism.
- Use Eysenck's arousal model of extraversion: introverts are chronically over-aroused and avoid external stimulation; extraverts are chronically under-aroused and seek stimulation.
- Model neuroticism as autonomic nervous-system reactivity to stress: low neuroticism equals stoic, high threshold for anxiety; high neuroticism equals easily rattled, low threshold.
- Apply Eysenck's PEN "super-trait" framework: Psychoticism, Extraversion, Neuroticism.
- Predict disorder expression from neuroticism level crossed with extraversion level: introverted neuroticism manifests as anxiety/depression/OCD/phobias; average extraversion as hysteria; high extraversion as psychopathy/delinquency.
- Treat psychoticism as a broad, loose factor combining low agreeableness and low conscientiousness, with lower internal consistency than extraversion or neuroticism.
- Deploy Gray's Behavioral Approach System (BAS): dopamine-mediated reward sensitivity that drives approach behavior, pleasure-seeking, and enthusiasm for incentives.
- Deploy Gray's Behavioral Inhibition System (BIS): threat/punishment sensitivity that triggers anxiety and behavioral withdrawal; modulated by serotonin and GABA.
- Use DeYoung's Big Five Aspect Scale hierarchy: two meta-traits—Stability (agreeableness, emotional stability, conscientiousness; serotonin-linked) and Plasticity (extraversion, openness; dopamine-linked).
- Link extraversion to the dopamine network: more dopamine receptors and stronger reward reactions to incentives.
- Link openness to dopamine-driven curiosity/novelty seeking, working memory, and reduced latent inhibition; note that openness as a whole factor does not directly correlate with dopamine.
- Connect agreeableness, emotional stability, and conscientiousness to serotonin; connect agreeableness specifically to lower baseline testosterone and larger dorsolateral prefrontal cortex volume.
- Connect neuroticism to amygdala overreactivity; further split neuroticism into withdrawal (linked to BIS) and volatility (linked to fight/flight/freeze responses).

## Failure modes named
- Eysenck fabricated some results and received tobacco-industry funding, so findings must be evaluated critically; common sense can flag biologically implausible claims.
- Psychoticism is internally weak because it merges two only modestly correlated traits (low agreeableness and low conscientiousness).
- Relying only on Big Five factor scores misses meaningful distinctions at the aspect level.
- Inferring a broad factor-level neurotransmitter correlation when only specific sub-characteristics show the link.
- Confusing correlation between questionnaire scores and neural measures with causal biological explanation.
