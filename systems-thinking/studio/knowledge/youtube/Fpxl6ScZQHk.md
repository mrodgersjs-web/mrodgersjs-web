# Mastering The Art of Evaluating Oil and Gas Deals

Promotional overview for a course on evaluating oil and gas deals, framing geology, petroleum systems, financial analysis, M&A, negotiation, and regulatory navigation as the core competencies for upstream deal-making.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Treat oil and gas deal evaluation as a cross-domain discipline combining geology, petroleum systems, and financial analysis.
- Begin technical evaluation with geological and petroleum-systems assessment to estimate resource potential.
- Translate technical estimates into deal economics through financial analysis.
- Evaluate exploration opportunities separately from production and acquisition opportunities.
- Approach acquisitions with a focus on reserves, assets, and operational integration.
- Structure deal-making around negotiation fundamentals and counterparty dynamics.
- Embed regulatory navigation as a continuous step within due diligence and execution.
- Screen opportunities by matching technical upside against market and regulatory constraints.
- Calibrate strategy to scale, from buying marginal stripper wells to building large integrated portfolios.
- Make decisions under dynamic market conditions rather than static assumptions.
