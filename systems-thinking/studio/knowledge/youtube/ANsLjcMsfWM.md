# 04 Types Of Centrality

This video introduces degree, closeness, and betweenness centrality as three distinct ways to measure a node's structural importance in a network.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Identify degree centrality by counting a node's edges or immediate neighbors as a measure of local connectivity.
- Measure closeness centrality by calculating how short a node's paths are to all other nodes in the network.
- Measure betweenness centrality by determining how frequently a node lies on the shortest paths between pairs of other nodes.
- Treat centrality as a multi-dimensional property rather than a single ranking of importance.
- Select the appropriate centrality metric based on whether local connectivity, global reach, or brokerage role matters most.
- Use these centrality measures to order and compare nodes in social network analysis.
