# Emergence – How Stupid Things Become Smart Together

Simple components following local rules interact to produce higher-level systems with novel properties that none of the individual parts possess.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Define emergence as complexity arising from simplicity through many local interactions under a shared ruleset.
- Apply the principle that "more is different" — aggregates gain properties absent from their components.
- Map layered emergence: atoms → molecules → proteins → cells → organs → organisms → societies.
- Use chemical signaling as a local communication protocol for role assignment and coordination.
- Employ feedback loops where individuals adjust behavior based on the frequency of encounters with other roles.
- Model cells as emergent structures from molecular interactions, with no central controller.
- Coordinate synchronization through neighbor-to-neighbor chemical communication rather than top-down command.
- Recognize emergent social entities as real and causally effective, even though they lack a physical body or fixed location.
- Distinguish emergent properties from their underlying parts, since zooming in can make the property disappear.

## Failure modes named
- Assuming a centralized commander where only local rules operate.
- Confusing reductionism with explanation and concluding the emergent property is unreal because it vanishes at small scales.
- Treating fluid emergent constructs as fixed, purely physical, or fully bounded.
- Believing complex structures require intentional design or planning to emerge.

## Studio relevance
02 frame - reframe scattered actors and local interactions as a higher-level system whose behavior cannot be predicted or controlled from individual parts alone.
