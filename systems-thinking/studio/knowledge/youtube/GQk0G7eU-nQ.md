# Mastering Pre Mortems:  Avoid Decision Disaster

The video teaches the premortem technique: imagining that a decision or plan has already failed, then working backward to identify causes and embed preventive measures before launch.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Define the decision or plan and establish a shared understanding of objectives, resources, and constraints before beginning.
- Adopt a temporary failure-focused mindset by fast-forwarding to a point where the plan has failed spectacularly.
- Individually and privately write down detailed, specific reasons the plan might fail, encouraging thoroughness and honesty.
- Create a safe space for independent critical thinking to overcome groupthink and reluctance to dissent.
- Share failure reasons with the group to cross-pollinate ideas and identify common themes and patterns.
- Map out potential failure chains and domino effects to see how small issues might escalate.
- Develop concrete preventative measures and mitigations for each identified risk.
- Adjust timelines, allocate resources, or build contingency plans based on the risks uncovered.
- Integrate the preventive measures directly into the final plan so insights are not lost.
- Treat the premortem as a proactive prevention tool rather than a prediction of the future.

## Failure modes named
- Groupthink and pressure to conform limiting risk identification.
- Reluctance to voice dissenting opinions during risk brainstorming.
- Optimism bias / focusing only on what could go right while ignoring what could go wrong.
- Being blindsided by unforeseen challenges because risks were never surfaced.
- Treating the premortem as fortune-telling rather than imagination-driven preparation.
- Dwelling on negativity without converting insights into preventive action.
- Letting premortem insights remain abstract and failing to integrate them into the plan.
