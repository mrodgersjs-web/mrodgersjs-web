# Validity of Tests & Measures - I/O Psychology Lecture 2 Part 6

This lecture explains how I/O psychologists validate tests and measures by defining job demands and performance criteria, then evaluating construct, criterion-related, and content-related validity.

## Procedure / models distilled
- Define validity as the accuracy and comprehensiveness of a test: does it measure what it claims to measure?
- Compute a validity coefficient, the correlation between a predictor and a criterion outcome such as job performance.
- Follow the four-step test validation sequence: conduct job analysis, define the performance criterion, create the predictor, and validate the predictor-criterion relationship.
- Use descriptive and inferential statistics to test the hypothesis of a strong, significant predictor-criterion relationship.
- Assess construct validity by measuring the overlap between a test and the underlying psychological construct it targets.
- Check convergent validity by correlating two measures of the same construct; they should converge on similar scores.
- Check discriminant validity by comparing correlations between related and unrelated constructs; related measures should correlate more strongly than unrelated ones.
- Apply criterion-related validity as the preferred legal and scientific evidence: correlate predictor scores with criterion scores.
- Establish predictive validity by administering the predictor at time 1, the criterion at time 2, and then correlating the two in a hired sample.
- Establish concurrent validity by administering the predictor and criterion at the same time to current employees when longitudinal validation is impractical.
- Evaluate content-related validity by having subject matter experts judge whether the test samples the work behaviors and KSAs required by the job.

## Failure modes named
- Weak construct validity: the test content has little overlap with the intended construct.
- Convergent validity failure: two measures of the same construct do not correlate as expected.
- Discriminant validity failure: unrelated measures correlate as strongly as related measures.
- Using a predictor for hiring before demonstrating predictive validity with actual job performance data.
- Including redundant predictors in a selection battery that waste resources without adding incremental validity.
- Confusing construct validity with content-related validity instead of distinguishing the theoretical construct from the job domain.
