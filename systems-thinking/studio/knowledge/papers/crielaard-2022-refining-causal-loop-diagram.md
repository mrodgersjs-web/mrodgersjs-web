# Refining the causal loop diagram: A tutorial for maximizing the contribution of domain expertise in computational system dynamics modeling.

## Claim

Applications of systems thinking usually stop at the conceptual causal loop diagram (CLD). That is not enough: to interpret a system's dynamics and run "what if" scenarios you must convert the CLD into a computable model — and expert mental models are the raw material for doing so rigorously.

## Method

Tutorial methodology: step-by-step procedures for capturing expert knowledge (eliciting mental models in biopsychosocial domains), translating it into CLD structure, then refining the CLD into a computational system dynamics model suitable for simulation and scenario analysis.

## Studio relevance

- **frame → diagnose**: the studio's causal-loop scaffolding is exactly the "stops at the CLD" stage this paper warns about; the tutorial is the bridge from a framed diagram to a runnable model.
- **leverage**: "what if" simulation over a refined CLD is how candidate interventions get tested before commitment.
- **ground**: 70-cite methodological citation for any claim that CLDs need computational refinement.
