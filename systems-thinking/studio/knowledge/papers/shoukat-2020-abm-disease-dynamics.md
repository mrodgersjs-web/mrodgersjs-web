# Agent-Based Modelling: An Overview with Application to Disease Dynamics

## Claim

The surge of interest in agent-based modelling rides on abundant complex data, but ABM practice is computationally sophisticated while theoretically shallow: the underlying theoretical basis is rarely used in model construction. A well-established quantitative method must rest on its theoretical frameworks for both construction and analysis.

## Method

Exposition/review: lay out the theoretical foundations of agent-based modelling and demonstrate their use in constructing and analyzing ABMs, with application to infectious-disease dynamics.

## Studio relevance

- **frame**: a rigor checklist before building any ABM — is this model grounded in theory, or just assembled because data and compute were available?
- **ground**: citable caution against data-rich, theory-poor simulation; pairs with Osoba 2020 (behavioral validity) as the two quality axes of an ABM.
- **diagnose**: disease-dynamics application is the canonical contact of ABM with stock-flow thinking (SIR stocks, individual-level emergence).
