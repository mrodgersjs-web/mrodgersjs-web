# System dynamics modeling for sustainable supply chain management: A literature review and systems thinking approach

## Claim

In sustainable supply chain management (SSCM), simulation is underrepresented relative to analytical models and mathematical programming — even though supply chains are archetypal complex dynamic systems where system dynamics simulation is the suitable tool for long-term, strategic decision-making under feedback, delays, and accumulation.

## Method

Literature review of SSCM-related system dynamics models, systematized through a systems-thinking conceptual framework, culminating in guidance (model classification + research directions) for future SD work in SSCM.

## Studio relevance

- **frame**: the conceptual framework is a worked example of scoping a messy real system (multi-stakeholder, environmental + social stocks) into a model boundary.
- **leverage**: the review's core lesson generalizes — strategic (structural) questions demand simulation, not optimization of a static snapshot.
- **ground**: highest-cited (270) empirical-methodological anchor in this library for SD-in-practice claims.
