# Complex Systems + Systems Engineering = Complex Systems Engineering

## Claim

A complex system is one in which phenomena emerge from multiscale interactions among components and their environments. The systems that systems engineers build — large numbers of concurrently operating, interacting, human and non-human components — increasingly share exactly those multiscale characteristics. Therefore systems engineering is becoming the engineering of complex systems, and needs complex-systems concepts (emergence, multiscale interaction) as first-class engineering material rather than as exceptions.

## Method

Definitional/position essay: precise definitions of complex system, Complex Systems field, and systems engineering, followed by the argument that their union is the natural description of modern engineered systems and an outline of what that union implies for practice.

## Studio relevance

- **frame**: supplies the canonical definition of "complex system" (multiscale interaction → emergence) the studio should use when deciding a target even *can* be treated with reductionist decomposition or needs systems treatment.
- **diagnose**: emergent misbehavior in engineered fleets (agent swarms, microservice meshes) is expected structure under this definition, not anomaly.
- **ground**: citable anchor for complexity vocabulary in engineering contexts.
