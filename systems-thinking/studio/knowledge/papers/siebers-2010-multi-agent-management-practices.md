# Multi-Agent Simulation and Management Practices

## Claim

Agent-based simulation (ABS) is a practical laboratory for understanding management practices and their link to organizational performance: best practices exist on paper, but applying them in a specific real organization is hard, and simulation lets practitioners test how practice changes propagate to performance before touching the live operation.

## Method

Multi-disciplinary modeling (economists, work psychologists, computer scientists): build intelligent-agent simulation models of store operations and management practices, developed and exercised with a well-known retail department store as the case.

## Studio relevance

- **diagnose**: template for grounding an org-performance diagnosis in a runnable model instead of opinion — the studio's "simulate before intervening" stance for human/organizational systems.
- **leverage**: candidate management-practice interventions can be trialed in the ABS; effects emerge from interactions, not from a single linear estimate.
- **frame**: shows how to encode an organization as agents + local rules when individual behavior is the unit that matters (vs. aggregate stocks).
