# Groping in the Dark: The First Decade of Global Modelling

## Core models

- **The first generation of global models, comparatively read**: World3 (Meadows et al., *The Limits to Growth*), Mesarovic–Pestel's World Integrated Model, the Bariloche Latin American model, MOIRA (food and agriculture), SARU, and the Leontief UN world model — one book setting their assumptions, structures, and conclusions side by side (an IIASA initiative bringing the model-builders themselves into dialogue).
- **Convergence-with-divergence as a finding**: the models differ in ideology, regions, and mechanisms, yet converge qualitatively — growth patterns run into physical and institutional constraints; delays make smooth transition harder than early action; "business as usual" produces strain somewhere in the world system. They diverge on where and when, which is exactly what their different structures imply.
- **Models as thinking instruments, not oracles**: global models exist to improve the user's mental models and to discipline debate about the future — numerical output is an argument scaffold, not a forecast. Their honest deliverable is insight into structure, not point predictions.
- **Groping as method**: under deep uncertainty, the rational strategy is incremental, adaptive, corrective movement — take a step, observe, correct — supported by models used continuously as early-warning and hypothesis-testing devices rather than one-shot planning machines.
- **Model limitations as first-class results**: data gaps, aggregation bias, omitted politics and human adaptability — the book treats the models' weaknesses as carefully as their conclusions, pioneering transparent model critique.

## Key definitions

- **Global model**: a computer simulation of interacting world-scale stocks and flows (population, capital, food, resources, pollution) used to explore long-run, society-wide consequences of policy assumptions.
- **Groping**: stepwise adaptive policy under uncertainty — deliberate small moves with feedback, in place of comprehensive control the system does not admit.
- **Scenario**: a coherent assumptions-set run through the model to ask "what would happen if," distinct from a prediction.

## The one diagram (in words)

A single standard reference run repeated as a fan of curves: population and industrial output rising together through the twentieth century, then — under unchanged assumptions — bending over somewhere in the twenty-first as resource, food, or pollution constraints bite; overlay each model's version and the bend appears in every one, at different dates and for different binding reasons.

## Three quotable ideas

1. "Groping" — under deep uncertainty, the rational strategy is small adaptive steps with continuous feedback, not grand plans.
2. The models disagree in detail and agree in shape: constraints, delays, and the premium on early smooth transition survive every structural variation.
3. Models are for improving mental models — their numbers are arguments about structure, not forecasts of the future.

## Studio relevance

- **ground**: the citable precedent for the studio's own epistemics — deterministic models as discipline for reasoning, with their limitations published alongside.
- **leverage**: "groping" is the intervention style implied by rank-9 delay reality: prefer reversible, instrumented steps over irreversible lurches.
- **frame**: the convergence-with-divergence finding is the template for multi-model framing — when independent structures agree qualitatively, believe the shape, not the date.
- **diagnose**: delay-premium logic (early smooth action beats late forced correction) is the diagnosed consequence in nearly every archetype.
