## Studio admission rule

A mechanism card enters this tranche only when its source hashes resolve, it has
at least two source classes, its proposed RIG process is allowlisted, and it names
feedback, leverage, failure signal, and falsifier. `validate.py` checks the registry
shape; the phase verifier checks its use in a run.
